Featured · LLM-explained anomaly detection

financial-anomaly-detector

Pulls OHLCV market data and flags anomalies with a Z-score and IQR ensemble over three signals. Claude then writes a two-sentence hypothesis and a High, Medium or Low risk tier for each flagged event.

ReturnsDaily log returns
VolumeVolume z-score, 20-day rolling
Volatility20-day realized volatility

flag when |z| > 2.5 AND the IQR fence is broken · rank by the summed |z| of the signals that fired

The output is one self-contained HTML dashboard, rebuilt and deployed by GitHub Actions. Without an API key every card falls back to a rule-based explanation. The live demo runs on 8 synthetic companies.

Featured · Agent safety testing

finagent-redteam

A stress-test harness for AI agents in regulated finance. It checks three things auditors ask about: prompt-injection resistance, unauthorized actions and hallucinated figures.

3/3Prompt injection
4/4Unauthorized actions
4/4Hallucinated figures

Live run on 24 Sep 2026 against gpt-oss-20b with two real MCP servers, one serving real EU and Romanian funding data. Verdicts grade the tool calls the model actually made, not what it says it did.

The first run scored 9 pass, 1 warn, 1 fail. Both non-passes were grader bugs, not model failures, and re-grading the saved transcripts gave 11 of 11. It is one run of one small model, not a benchmark.

bidscout

Should you bid on this Romanian public tender? It reads each new notice from the SEAP / SICAP portal, compares the buyer's conditions with a company profile and answers GO, CHECK or NO-GO.

The LLM only extracts facts, and every fact keeps the buyer's own sentence as its source quote. The verdict comes from a rules engine written in YAML. When unsure it says CHECK, never NO-GO. The ingestion client guards against the portal's silent failures, such as ignored filters and capped results, and each trap is a test.

propaganda-signature-detection

A TF-IDF logistic regression trained on 36 Cold War documents, then run over 17 contemporary US speeches, with a seven-device rhetoric lexicon alongside.

Every modern speech scored 0.69 to 0.75, close to the Soviet mean of 0.76, yet enemy-naming and us-versus-them framing are what modern speech uses least. The write-up lists its own limits.
